# Changelog

## 0.2.2

- Expanded Marketplace search keywords for better discoverability.

## 0.2.1

- Added screenshots to the README.

## 0.2.0

- Added five new aircraft: Eurofighter Typhoon, SEPECAT Jaguar, MiG-21, MiG-29, and Mirage 2000.
- Resized and optimized all sprite assets, and removed opaque backgrounds from the new aircraft's artwork.
- Flight paths are now linear on both axes, with jets reversing vertical direction at the top/bottom margins instead of drifting on a sine wave.
- Added formation flying (V, line abreast, echelon, diamond, trail) via a new title-bar command, with wingmen tracking the lead jet.

## 0.1.0

- Initial release: six-jet squadron (F-16, F-22, F/A-18, Su-57, Su-30, Su-25) with add/remove, afterburner, and day/night sky toggle.
